\textbf{Interpretation:} The traditional approach requires approximately 155 hours (nearly 4 weeks of full-time work) to complete a single IE research paper that meets top-tier publication standards. SynthIE reduces this to 21 hours (less than 3 days), representing an 86\% time savings. Critically, this acceleration does not compromise quality; rather, it enhances quality by ensuring that every phase adheres to the ``Triad of Rigor'' (Operational, Economic, Safety validation) that human researchers might inadvertently omit under time pressure. For an IE department producing 20 papers per year, SynthIE saves approximately 2,680 hours annually, equivalent to 1.3 full-time researcher positions.
